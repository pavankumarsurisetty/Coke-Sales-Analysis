# 🥤 Coca-Cola Sales Analysis Using Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Coca-Cola sales data across different retailers and regions in the United States using Microsoft Excel.

The objective is to evaluate sales performance, identify profitable retailers, understand regional sales trends, and generate meaningful business insights to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze total sales and units sold.
- Identify top-performing retailers.
- Compare sales performance across different regions.
- Analyze beverage brand performance.
- Evaluate operating profit and operating margins.
- Identify monthly sales trends.
- Provide actionable business recommendations.

## 📊 Dataset Information

The dataset contains 3,888 records and 13 columns covering sales transactions across the United States during 2021.

Key columns include:

- Retailer
- Retailer ID
- Invoice Date
- Region
- State
- City
- Beverage Brand
- Price per Unit
- Units Sold
- Total Sales
- Operating Profit
- Operating Margin
- Month

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Excel Formulas
- Data Cleaning
- Data Visualization
- Slicers and Filters

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

1. Dataset exploration and understanding.
2. Data cleaning and preparation.
3. Retailer-wise sales analysis.
4. Region-wise sales analysis.
5. Beverage brand performance analysis.
6. Monthly sales trend analysis.
7. Profitability and operating margin analysis.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators

- Total Sales
- Total Units Sold
- Total Operating Profit
- Average Operating Margin

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Focus on high-performing retailers to maintain revenue growth.
- Identify opportunities in underperforming regions.
- Optimize pricing strategies to improve profitability.
- Monitor monthly sales trends for better inventory planning.
- Evaluate beverage brand performance to improve sales strategies.
